The problem test

A good idea has a clear shape.

Use these six checks before you fall in love with a solution.

01

A clear user

Someone specific needs the result.

02

A recurring trigger

The work happens often enough to matter.

03

Safe inputs

Useful data exists and can be accessed responsibly.

04

A judgment

Something is classified, transformed, or decided.

05

A visible action

The output moves real work forward.

06

A measurable change

Time, quality, error rate, or experience improves.

Where to look

Bring two ideas.
We will help you choose one.

01

Look for work that keeps returning to you.

  • What requires collecting information from several places?
  • What do you postpone because it takes too much context switching?
  • Which recurring decision uses roughly the same inputs?
  • What could AI prepare while you retain the final call?

Weekly leadership brief

Collect team metrics, open decisions, customer signals, and missed commitments. Prepare a one-page brief that highlights what needs attention next week.
Success90 min → 15 min

Meeting follow-through

After every important meeting, turn notes into decisions, owners, deadlines, and follow-up drafts for my approval.
SuccessFollow-ups out the same day

Decision inbox

Group messages that need my judgment, bring in the missing context, and prepare a short recommendation without replying on my behalf.
SuccessOne focused review queue

Calendar preparation

Before each external or leadership meeting, assemble the relationship history, latest metrics, unresolved questions, and a suggested agenda.
SuccessPrepared before every meeting

Commitment review

Every Friday, find commitments I made across meetings and messages, show what is open, and prepare the next follow-up.
SuccessNo important loose ends

Metrics exception brief

Watch the metrics I care about, explain unusual movement, and bring me only the exceptions that need investigation or a decision.
SuccessAttention goes to exceptions

Research digest

Turn saved articles, reports, and competitor updates into a weekly digest with implications, open questions, and ideas worth testing.
Success2 hr → 20 min

Recurring communication

Use current metrics, decisions, and project updates to prepare the first draft of my weekly team note in my usual structure.
SuccessFirst draft ready on time

02

Look for work that slows down at a handoff.

  • Where is information repeatedly copied between systems?
  • Where do people classify, validate, reconcile, or chase status?
  • Where does quality depend on one person remembering the process?
  • Where could AI recommend an action while a human approves?
Ideas to borrow
1 / 8

Escalation routing

When a high-risk escalation arrives, gather the case context, group the root cause, recommend an owner, and draft a status update for approval.
SuccessNo high-risk case missed

Claims reliability watch

Detect stalled or inconsistent claims, explain the likely failure point, and create an investigation queue with the right owner.
SuccessIssues found before complaints

Renewal risk briefing

Combine usage, service, relationship, and commercial signals to identify accounts at risk and prepare the next-best action for review.
SuccessEvery risk has an owner

Benefits onboarding

Turn a signed customer handoff into a complete launch plan, missing-information checklist, owner map, and employee communication drafts.
SuccessFewer launch-day surprises

Hiring feedback loop

Collect interview notes, flag missing evidence, prepare a role-specific summary, and route the final decision to the hiring owner.
SuccessDecision within 24 hours

Incident communication

When an incident begins, assemble verified facts, maintain a timeline, and prepare audience-specific updates with explicit approval gates.
SuccessClear updates without guesswork

Implementation handoff

Translate sales commitments and customer context into milestones, risks, dependencies, and a kickoff brief the delivery team can use.
SuccessNo context lost at handoff

Reconciliation exceptions

Compare records across systems, isolate mismatches, suggest likely causes, and send only unresolved exceptions to an operator.
SuccessHumans review exceptions only
